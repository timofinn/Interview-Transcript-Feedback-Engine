2025–2026 | The Feedback Loop Problem

Tim Finnigan · Pinole Valley High School · TIP Year 2 · CSTP 5

🔍

The Problem Identified

Feedback loops were too slow. By the time students got a response on their writing, the moment had passed. The goal: close the gap between submission and insight.

🔍
Fall '25
🤖

Enter: Classroom Companion

An AI-powered feedback tool. Students submit drafts, get near-instant rubric-based feedback, then revise. Promising in theory — and genuinely useful in practice, when it worked.

🤖
Oct '25
🚧

The Paywall Problem

Monthly subscription. Teacher salary. You do the math. Ultimately unsustainable — had to jump ship. The tool showed real promise, but the model wasn't built for educators without a budget.

🚧
Nov '25
🛠️

Fine. I'll Build It.

Developed the Transcript Analyzer from scratch — a custom tool that reads student interview transcripts and tags question types across three levels of inquiry. Level 1 ice-breakers through Level 3 reflective, meaning-making questions.

🛠️
Dec '25–Feb '26
🐛

Debug. Push. Repeat.

Hours of regex pattern-matching. Mock transcripts. Broken builds. Commits and patches. The gap between "it works" and "it actually works with real student submissions" turned out to be substantial.

🐛
Feb–Mar '26
🚀

Deployed with Students

Ran the Analyzer at the end of Unit 6. Students got annotated transcripts with scored question levels. Immediately noticed: most scrolled straight to their score. Back to the drawing board on presentation — but the engine worked.

🚀
Apr '26
💡

Sometimes the Best Tool Is You

Live, on-the-spot verbal demonstrations. Student explains the concept. Teacher responds in real time. Feedback loop: immediate. Cost: zero. The gap closes the second they try it out loud and hear where they went wrong.
