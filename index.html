Analysis is pattern-based and should be reviewed with your teacher. Question-level classification (L1–L3) is a starting point for conversation, not a verdict. Move taxonomy follows the Verbal & Structural Cues reference sheet: verbal moves (Paraphrasis, Follow-up, Personal Connection, Reflection/Naming, Nudge of Encouragement, Summarizing, Asking for Specific Memory) and structural moves (On-Ramp, Circling Back, Smooth Bridge, Skilled Pivot).
